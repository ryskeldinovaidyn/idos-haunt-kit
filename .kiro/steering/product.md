# Product: Haunt Kit by iDos Games

Haunt Kit is iDos Games' entry for Kiroween 2026 (category: Skeleton Crew).

- iDos Games (idosgames.com) is an AI platform where anyone creates games, apps and AI tools, publishes them on idosgames.com and can launch an optional token on Solana, BNB or Base.
- Haunt Kit is a lean starter template ("the skeleton") for building web apps on the iDos Games SDK, plus two very different apps built from it:
  1. Grave Runner: a Halloween endless-runner game (canvas).
  2. Séance: an AI tool where a spirit answers your question and paints its own portrait.
- Goal: prove that one small, well-documented skeleton can power both a game and an AI tool, and that a new app can start from it in minutes.
- Audience: indie developers and creators who want to ship a web game or AI app with sign-in, a wallet, leaderboards and AI without writing a backend.
- Tone: spooky, playful, polished. Every screen should feel intentional and Halloween-themed, while staying readable and accessible.
